# Content polish design

## Goal

Turn the owner-edited draft into a coherent, publishable portfolio without changing its restrained editorial-cosmos direction or inventing personal claims.

## Content hierarchy

The Lab keeps its card structure, but long implementation descriptions become labeled blocks with deliberate paragraph spacing. Completed Vision Board and Efficiency content loses placeholder styling, while projects that still lack substantiated detail remain visible only at summary level. Empty future slots and comic placeholders are hidden so the page reads as intentionally curated rather than unfinished.

## Typography

Each project card uses one sans-serif body system for summaries, metadata, and expanded details. Serif typography remains limited to names, section headings, and editorial numbering. The delivery matrix uses the same body family in all columns; hierarchy comes from weight and color instead of switching the first column to a different typeface.

## Personal positioning

The third Advantages row replaces third-party praise with three owner-defined keywords: learning ability, responsibility, and agency. Two hidden reserve items keep the visual editor's existing content-key sequence stable, preventing the owner's saved edits from being remapped to unrelated sections.

## Deliverables

The four rows are replaced with the owner's exact categories:

1. Content strategy and knowledge products
2. AI products and workflows
3. Cross-cultural expression and localization
4. Overseas market and product marketing

## Writing data

Because no verified audience or engagement metrics exist in the project, the writing card shows only evidence-backed editorial data: eight selected samples, six stated themes, and an ongoing publishing status. This leaves a clear visual pattern that can later hold verified followers, reads, saves, or conversion data without fabricating performance.

## Images and verification

The owner-provided VB files are normalized to the evidence loader's `visionboard` key, and the Efficiency screenshot keeps its existing key. Captions describe only what is visible. Verification covers image discovery, text-key stability, responsive typography, automated integration tests, and real-browser screenshots.
